Under uvicorn the loop is the lever: on ringloop it serves 1.9 times the
requests it serves on uvloop, for 0.6 of the CPU per request.

Granian parses HTTP and does the socket I/O in Rust threads; one worker uses
about 1.5 cores and serves twice what uvicorn serves on ringloop, for about
0.8 of the CPU per request. The event loop only carries its callbacks:
ringloop, rloop and uvloop are level there, and all three are well ahead
of asyncio's loop.
