Small responses: about half the CPU per request of uvloop (3.3 to 3.9 µs
against 7.7 to 8.0), on both backends; the req/s column of the plaintext
rows is noisy in this run. A rendered page is the application's work and
the loop makes no difference.

For scale, servers.txt has Granian with a hello-world ASGI app at about
185,000 requests per second on 1.5 cores (7.9 µs per request); Stario on
ringloop serves its JSON case at 200,000 to 230,000 on one core.

Podman's default seccomp profile (containers-common, podman 5.7) lists no
io_uring system call: in a default container a loop uses the epoll backend.
